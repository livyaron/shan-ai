{# חדר מבצעים — "לוח מצב": the wall display. Sized to be read from ~4m on a TV. Shows the WHOLE active board by rotating through pages of cards, colours every card by time-to-target, carries the latest status update on each card plus a board-wide update ticker, and holds no write actions at all — a screen that talks, not a screen you work in. The whole page is exactly one viewport high and never scrolls: every row is sized in vh and the card area takes what is left, so a 1080p TV, a 4K panel and a laptop all show the SAME six cards. A TV browser has no scrollbar and nobody walks up to a wall screen to scroll it — anything below the fold is simply lost, which is what the old fixed-height cards did to half the board. /dashboard/war-room?style=wall — add &tv=1 for a chrome-free kiosk screen. #} {# Reloads only at the END of a full rotation cycle: a fixed 60s refresh used to cut the cycle short, so the later pages were never seen. #} חדר מבצעים · לוח מצב — SHAN-AI {# No app navbar here on purpose: on a wall screen the top row must be the board itself, and every row of chrome costs a mission card. #}
